Privacy Policy

What We Collect

We only collect information you choose to share with us through our contact form: your name, email address, phone number, and any details you include about your event.

How We Use It

Your information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and discuss your event. We do not sell, share, or distribute your personal data to third parties.

Cookies

This website uses a single cookie to remember your cookie consent preference (accept or decline). This cookie is stored locally in your browser and contains no personal information.

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics tools that identify you.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you by emailing hello@aurascape.in. We will respond within 7 days.
