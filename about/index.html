Our Story

About Aura & Scape

Two Friends, One Dream

Aura & Scape was born from a simple belief: every celebration should feel like a story worth remembering. We're Antima and Shilpa — two friends who found their calling in the art of transformation.

What started as decorating a friend's intimate gathering grew into a shared passion for crafting spaces that don't just look beautiful — they make you feel something.

Today, we pour that same care into every event we touch. Whether it's a grand wedding or a quiet anniversary, we bring the same energy: attention to detail, a love of colour, and a genuine desire to see your face when you walk into the space we've created.

What Guides Us

Dream First

We start with your vision, not our templates. Your event should feel unmistakably yours.

Decorate with Heart

Every petal, every light, every drape is placed with intention. Nothing is arbitrary. Everything is felt.

Celebrate Fully

We handle the logistics so you can be fully present in your celebration. You live the moment. We guard the details.

How We Got Here

The Spark

Antima decorated Shilpa's sister's engagement on a tight budget. The photos went viral in their friend group. The next request came that weekend.

Finding Our Voice

Three wedding seasons in, we realised our style: soft pastels, meaningful touches, and a refusal to copy the "standard" look. Clients started asking for us by name.

Aura & Scape is Born

We stopped doing favours and started doing this full-time. A name that captures what we do: the aura we create, the scape we build around it.

Now

A small team with big ambitions. Every event still gets our full attention. We'd rather do 20 events beautifully than 100 events well-enough.

Let's Create Together

Every great event starts with a conversation. Tell us what you're dreaming of.
